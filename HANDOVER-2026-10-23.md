# Ownly Together: handover (23 Oct 2026, night)

## What it is
Group ordering for Ownly (Rapido's zero-commission food app). It's the Class 29 (No-code / Airtable) assignment, done in Supabase + GitHub instead. Sameer will pitch it to Rapido with his resume. It's branded as Ownly, with a "Concept prototype by Sameer Mittimani. Not an official Rapido product." strip on every screen. Payments are demo only, with a warning on the pay screen.

## Rules Sameer set (do not change without asking)
- Ask before adding any rule, limit or feature he didn't state. He was angry when I invented a 6-person / ₹2,000 cap.
- Group order: up to **12 people**. No order-value cap. **Unlimited sign-ups**, with no demo accounts. Anyone signs up with **email OTP**.
- Split: **own dishes at full price; dishes marked shared ÷ everyone in the group; Rapido charges (delivery + packaging + taxes) ÷ everyone.** Everyone, the host included, pays on the demo payment screen.
- Profile: name, phone, addresses labelled home / office / friend / other, each with a GPS pin (after consent).
- Payment info: masked labels and gateway tokens only. Never card numbers, CVVs or UPI PINs.
- Not yet confirmed with Sameer (kept as built): only the host locks, places or cancels; one restaurant per group order.
