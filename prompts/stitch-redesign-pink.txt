==================== PROMPT 1 (mobile, 390 px) ====================

Design Ownly Together, a group food-ordering feature for Ownly (Rapido's zero-commission food app), launching in Chennai. Follow the attached design system exactly and use the attached Ownly logo. The attached implementation plan has the full product rules.

What it does: one person (the host) starts a group order and shares a link. Friends, flatmates or colleagues join, add their own food, and each person pays only their own share. Nobody has to chase anyone on UPI.

LOOK AND FEEL
- Ownly pink: #F50062 for headlines and accents. Buttons are #C40054 with white text, hover #A80047. Pale pink #FFE3ED for chips, blush #FFF1F6 for alternate sections, page #FFF9FC, white cards.
- Navy #111D35 for text, #576177 for secondary text, borders #F0D7E2.
- Status chips always have words, not just colour: Paid (green #16845A), Pending (amber #A65D00), Payment failed (red), Order locked.
- Fonts: Plus Jakarta Sans for headings (700–800), Inter for everything else. Use tabular numbers for every ₹ amount.
- Rounded cards (16–24 px corners), pill buttons, soft shadows, lots of white space. Lucide-style line icons. Playful, frictionless, trustworthy.
- Real Chennai food photos (dosa, idli, biryani, Chettinad, filter coffee) and prices in ₹.
- Every screen has a thin strip: "Concept prototype by Sameer Mittimani. Not an official Rapido product."

SCREENS (mobile, 390 px wide)
1. Sign in: email, then a 6-digit code sent to that email. No passwords. First-time users add their name.
2. Explore restaurants: Chennai area chips (OMR, Velachery, T. Nagar, Adyar, Anna Nagar, Nungambakkam, Mylapore), cuisine filter, veg-only toggle, restaurant cards with photo, cuisine and area.
3. Menu: dishes with photo, price, veg / non-veg mark, Add button. Unavailable dishes greyed out with "Unavailable".
4. Start group order (host): pick restaurant, delivery address, cut-off time (15 min, 30 min, custom) and the split rule: "Pay for what you ordered" (default) or "Split everything equally". Then show the room code (e.g. TNR742), a QR code, "Copy link" and "Share on WhatsApp".
5. Join: open the link or type the code. Preview the restaurant, host name, cut-off and split rule, then "Join group".
6. Shared cart: dishes grouped by person with notes, a "Shared" toggle on a dish, member status (Adding items, Done), countdown to cut-off, "4 of 12 joined", running total.
7. Host checkout: lock the cart, enter Rapido's delivery fee, packaging and taxes, see each person's share, "Place order", "Cancel order".
8. Order confirmation: a small celebration, who ordered what, each person's share with Paid / Pending, and the tracking link with "Copy link" and "Share on WhatsApp".
9. My share and pay: my dishes, my share of shared dishes, my share of Rapido's charges, my total, how it was worked out for the chosen split rule. "Pay my share" opens a Razorpay-style demo sheet: UPI apps, QR, card, netbanking, "Paid the host in cash", plus "Simulate success" and "Simulate failure". Note: "Demo payment: no real money moves." Below: the group's Paid / Pending list.
10. Live tracking: Order accepted → Preparing → Picked up → On the way → Delivered, a simple Chennai map, "Your Rapido Captain is on the way".
11. Order history: past group orders with restaurant, date, people, my share, status, receipt, "Order again with this group".
12. Profile: name, phone (optional), saved addresses (Home, Office, Friend's place, Other) with a map pin after the user allows location, saved payment methods as masked labels only (e.g. "Visa •••• 4242").
13. States: group full ("This group is full (12 people)"), link expired, dish unavailable, payment failed with Retry, empty cart.

RULES (follow exactly)
- Up to 12 people per group, host included. No limit on order value. One restaurant per group. Only the host can lock, place or cancel.
- Room code is 6 capital letters or numbers with no dash (TNR742). Same code in share and WhatsApp messages.
- Rapido's delivery, packaging and taxes are plain amounts split equally across everyone.
- Everyone pays their own share, the host included.
- Chennai places and restaurants only.
- Do NOT include: price comparisons with other apps, "markup", "₹0 fees", "you saved" banners, "absorbed fees", a bag capacity gauge, any 6-person or ₹2,000 limit, rider names, number plates or a call button, made-up product names like "Rapido Direct" or "Express Saver", company names, CIN or GSTIN numbers, or made-up statistics.
- Footer text only: "Concept prototype by Sameer Mittimani. Not an official Rapido product."
