groups Ownly Together Protocol

Group Lunch, minus the Excel sheets & awkward GPay reminders.

Ordering with friends at your apartment, hostel, or IT park? Open an Ownly room. Everyone picks their dish on their own phone, pays their own individual share via UPI, and meals arrive in one bundled order with zero delivery surcharge.

1

Pick Restaurant & Set Cut-Off

Host selects the kitchen and sets an order timer (e.g. 15 mins). A unique 6-character room code like TNR742 is created (supports up to 12 members).

2

Colleagues Add Dishes Directly

Friends tap the link, select their own food with custom instructions (extra gravy, less spicy), and see live room updates.

3

Split UPI & Single Rapid Delivery

Everyone pays their exact portion via individual UPI QR. Rapido captain delivers the consolidated order to your lobby.
