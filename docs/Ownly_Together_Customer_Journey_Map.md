## 2. User's product brief — response (verbatim)

**Product:** Ownly Together. Group food ordering for Ownly, Rapido’s zero-commission food app. One person starts an order, friends join with a link and add their own food, and the app splits the bill so everyone pays only their share.

**Goal:** Adoption. Ownly isn’t in Chennai yet, and Ownly Together is how it launches there. Every group order brings new people into the app through the share link, up to 11 others per order. Engagement is the second goal: repeat group orders with “Order again with this group”. The north star is group orders completed per week.

**Core problem:** When a group orders food together, one person does all the work outside the app:

- they collect everyone’s choices on WhatsApp;
- they rebuild the cart by hand and pay the full bill;
- they spend the next day or two chasing people for their share on UPI.

Choices get lost, late additions come in after the order is placed, and the host often never gets all their money back.

**Region/persona:** Chennai, starting with areas full of office teams and young people living away from home: OMR, Velachery, T. Nagar, Adyar, Anna Nagar, Nungambakkam and Mylapore.

- **Primary (the host):** the office “lunch organiser” or the flatmate who always places the order. Wants less coordination work and to get paid back in full.
- **Secondary (the members):** colleagues, flatmates and hostel friends. They want to pick quickly, see what others ordered and pay only their share.
- **Key traits:**
  - price-aware;
  - use UPI every day;
  - already plan orders in WhatsApp groups;
  - order with others often, for office lunches, late-night flat orders and weekend get-togethers;
  - comfortable with apps, so they’ll try a new food app if a friend’s link brings them in.

## 3. Assistant's journey-map response (verbatim)

## Customer Journey Map

**Product:** Ownly Together | **Market:** Chennai | **Goal:** Adoption → Engagement

| Row \ Stage | Awareness | Consideration | Onboarding | Retention | Loyalty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Customer actions** | Receive WhatsApp invite; open link | Compare menus, prices, payment options | Join group; add food; pay UPI | Rejoin group; reorder lunch | Invite colleagues; share group links |
| **Touchpoints** | WhatsApp groups; Ownly invite link | Group preview; restaurant menu | Ownly app; shared cart; UPI | Order history; saved groups | WhatsApp; group invitation screen |
| **Experience** | Curious: “What's Ownly Together?” | Hopeful: “Everyone pays separately?” | Relieved: “Only paying my share!” | Convenient: “Same group, fewer taps.” | Proud: “Lunch sorted, no chasing!” |
| **Pain points** | Unfamiliar brand: “Is this reliable?” | Price uncertainty: “Any hidden charges?” | Installation friction: “Another app?” | Repeated coordination: “Invite everyone again?” | Limited incentives: “Why share again?” |
| **Opportunities (HMW)** | How might we build instant trust? | How might we show transparent pricing? | How might we simplify invitation onboarding? | How might we enable instant reordering? | How might we encourage organic referrals? |

## Top 4 Recommendations (1-liners, prioritized)

1. **WhatsApp deep-link onboarding** to fix installation friction @ Onboarding ⇒ **Invite-to-signup conversion** (Effort: M)
2. **Live shared cart with individual totals** to fix price uncertainty @ Consideration ⇒ **Cart-join rate** (Effort: M)
3. **UPI split-pay checkout** to fix host payment burden @ Onboarding ⇒ **Group orders completed/week** (Effort: L)
4. **Order again with this group** to fix repeated coordination @ Retention ⇒ **Repeat group orders/week** (Effort: M)

## Journey Snapshot

WhatsApp chaos → effortless split payments → habitual group ordering.

- **Shapes:** Rounded cards, generous whitespace, soft shadows, friendly modern sans-serif typography, curved connectors and small delivery/food/cart icons.
- **Voice:** Warm, clear, transparent pricing, group convenience and control. Avoid suggesting that Ownly Together is live.
- **Verified Hyderabad-page themes:** No packaging fees, no platform fees, no surge charges and transparent pricing. These are current Ownly brand themes, **not independently verified requirements of the proposed Chennai group feature**.
- **Chennai cues:** Office lunch on OMR, Velachery flatmates, WhatsApp invite, familiar UPI split-payment flow. Keep local cues subtle, avoid clichés.
