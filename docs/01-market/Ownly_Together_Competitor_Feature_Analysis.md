# Ownly Together — Competitor & Feature Analysis

**Market:** Chennai, India  
**As of:** 24 October 2026  
**Company:** Ownly (Rapido-associated food ordering business)  
**Product:** Ownly Together — *proposed* group-ordering feature; not a confirmed live Chennai offering  
**Objective:** Adoption first; repeat group orders second  
**North Star:** Completed group orders per week

> **Positioning:** **Order Together. Pay Your Share. No More Chasing.**  
> The strategic concept: a host shares an invite link (including through WhatsApp), members add their own food, and each pays only their share by UPI. Groups can reorder together later.

## 1. Competitor landscape

| Competitor | Type | Positioning / strengths | Gap or opportunity for Ownly Together |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Swiggy** | Direct | Established food marketplace; group cart via link/QR; multi-restaurant ordering from up to five restaurants in one checkout. | Member-by-member checkout payment **not verified**; Ownly can emphasise payment ownership and host relief. |
| **Zomato** | Direct | Group carts through invite links; scheduled orders; broad food marketplace. | Individual group checkout payment **not verified**; separate-payment proposition needs hands-on validation. |
| **EatSure** | Direct / adjacent | Real-time shared group cart, multi-restaurant choices, member-labelled items and group discounts. | Publicly described flow has the **host finalise and pay**; same-group one-tap reorder is not provided in published FAQ. |
| **WhatsApp + UPI apps** | Indirect / status quo | Familiar, inexpensive social coordination and peer-to-peer transfers. | Order selections, cart building, and collection happen across separate manual steps. |

## 3. Five competitor-inspired feature opportunities

| Priority | Feature | Inspiration | User benefit | Suggested metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | QR join alongside WhatsApp invite link | Swiggy | Fast joining at office tables | Invite-to-join conversion |
| **P1** | Member payment-status view and reminders | UPI app payment-request patterns | Host knows who has paid | Fully paid group-order rate |
| **P2** | Scheduled office-lunch orders | Zomato | Plan lunch before peak hours | Scheduled group orders/week |
| **P2** | Multi-restaurant collaborative checkout | EatSure + Swiggy | Accommodate different food preferences | Group cart completion rate |
| **P3** | Transparent group savings/rewards | EatSure | Encourage trial and repeat use | 30-day group repeat rate |

## 4. Executive summary

1. **Shared carts are table stakes.** Swiggy, Zomato and EatSure already support group cart creation.
2. **Differentiate on payments.** Individual UPI checkout could remove the host's collection burden; validate competitor checkout flows before calling it exclusive.
3. **Make joining effortless.** WhatsApp invites and QR joining should drive member acquisition without heavy friction.
4. **Build repeat behaviour.** Save groups and streamline reorders; optimise for **completed group orders per week**.
