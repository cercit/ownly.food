# Ownly Together — Chennai Market Opportunity

**Date:** October 2026  
**Product:** Ownly Together — group food ordering with individual UPI payments  
**Industry:** Online food delivery / FoodTech  
**Region:** Chennai  
**Audience:** Product team and cross-functional stakeholders  
**Approach:** Hybrid evidence-based model using published benchmarks + clearly labelled assumptions

---

## Summary
This model estimates the launch opportunity for **Ownly Together** in Chennai using both **customer numbers** and **financial value (GMV)**. It separates **GMV** from **Ownly revenue** and does **not assume an unverified monetisation model**.

The model is intended as a **planning hypothesis**, not a final forecast.

---

### Interpretation
- **TAM** = Total potential group-order users in Chennai.
- **SAM** = Reachable users in the initial launch clusters.
- **SOM** = Year-one realistic adoption target.

## Formula Logic
- **TAM users** = Population × age share × delivery participation × group-order participation
- **SAM users** = TAM users × launch-area coverage
- **SOM users** = SAM users × adoption rate
- **Annual GMV** = (active users ÷ group size) × weekly order frequency × basket value × 52

---

## Key Insights
1. **Meaningful niche opportunity:** Chennai group ordering is a smaller but valuable wedge inside the broader food-delivery market.
2. **Adoption matters most:** Invite-to-signup conversion and repeat group orders will strongly determine success.
3. **Clear differentiator:** Shared carts are common; individual UPI payments and repeat groups are stronger differentiators.
4. **Biggest risks:** Actual group-order frequency, restaurant coverage, payment completion and retention are still unvalidated.

---

## Important Notes
- **GMV is not revenue.** This analysis does not estimate Ownly revenue.
- Figures are **illustrative planning estimates** based on secondary benchmarks and explicit assumptions.
- The next step is to validate the high-impact assumptions through Chennai interviews, surveys and pilot launch data.
