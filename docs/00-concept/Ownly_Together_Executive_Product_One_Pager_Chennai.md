# Ownly Together — Executive Product One-Pager

**Decision:** **PROCEED WITH VALIDATION** — discovery and payment feasibility only; full build not approved.  
**Market:** Chennai, India · **Assessment:** 24 October 2026  
**Product:** Ownly Together, a proposed group food-ordering capability for Ownly.  
**Tagline:** *Order Together. Pay Your Share. No More Chasing.*  
**Evidence labels:** Owner observation; externally documented facts; unvalidated assumptions; recommendations.

## 1. Executive Summary

Proposed for Chennai: **one shared order, up to 12 people, individual payments**. A host invites a group and selects a fair splitting method; members add food and pay their shares. Intended impact: invite-led acquisition, less host effort and more repeat orders. **Decision: validate, not full-build approval.**

## 2. Problem

**User:** office lunch organisers and flatmates. **Root cause:** WhatsApp choices, cart, checkout and repayment live in separate places. **Impact:** manual copying, errors, upfront expense and awkward UPI chasing. This is **owner observation**, not yet measured Chennai demand.

## 3. Opportunity

Prior planning scenario: **TAM 6.05L**, **SAM 1.51L** potential participants; **SOM 10,584** target users / **₹11.35 Cr** annualised GMV. All rely on unvalidated behaviour and reach assumptions; GMV **is not revenue**. Chennai supply and adoption need verification.

## 4. Proposed Solution

**Invite:** host creates a group and shares a link.  
**Choose:** members add dishes to one cart.  
**Allocate:** host selects own-items-plus-shared or equal split.  
**Settle:** members pay independently; statuses and history are visible.

## 5. Target User

**Primary:** host seeking effortless group coordination and settlement.  
**Secondary:** member seeking food choice, fair totals and independent payment.  
**Alternative:** WhatsApp + UPI or competitor group carts. Both must complete checkout.

## 6. Why This Could Win

Swiggy and Zomato already offer group carts [2,3]. The opportunity is **transparent individual settlement and easy same-group reorder**, not a novel shared cart. Ownly may benefit from Rapido distribution and restaurant relationships [6], but durable defensibility is **unproven**.

## 7. Business Impact

Measure **incremental first paid orders** from invites and **repeat completed groups**, then assess contribution margin after additional payment, support, refund and delivery costs. A higher GMV figure alone does not justify investment; zero-commission positioning limits assumed revenue streams [1,6].

## 8. Success Metrics

**North Star:** completed group orders/week.  
**Drivers:** invite→first paid order; group checkout completion; D28 repeat-group rate; incremental contribution margin.  
**Guardrails:** settlement mismatches, payment failures, disputes/support volume.

## 9. MVP & Scope

**In:** ≤12 incl. host; email-code sign-in; one restaurant; shared cart; two split rules; computed charges; individual payments; cash status; user-immutable audit history.  
**Out:** multi-restaurant orders, loyalty, scheduling, advanced offers. **No sensitive payment credentials stored.**

## 10. Key Risks & Assumptions

**Critical:** approved 12-person collection, reconciliation and restaurant settlement may not be feasible or economical [4,5].  
**Pricing:** PRD packaging ₹15/₹30/dish contradicts Ownly's no-packaging-fee promise [1].  
**Adoption:** email-code friction, restaurant coverage and repeat use remain unknown.  
**Cash:** 'received by host' must not equal 'settled with restaurant'.

## 11. Recommendation

**PROCEED WITH VALIDATION.**  
1. Interview **6 hosts + 6 members** in Chennai.  
2. Test invitation, cart and payment prototype.  
3. Secure provider/legal approval for settlement flow.  
4. Resolve packaging fee policy and model incremental costs.  
**Gate:** small real-order pilot only after these pass; stop if payments or economics fail.
